Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Data We Collect

To provide our services, Spendly collects the following information:

  • Account Information: Your full name and email address provided during registration.
  • Financial Data: Expense amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions that you manually enter into the app.
  • Technical Data: Basic log data and session information to maintain the security and stability of the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain your personal expense dashboard.
  • To manage your user account and authenticate your sessions.
  • To improve our app's functionality and user experience.
  • To communicate with you regarding service updates or account issues.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in our database. We implement standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal data to outside parties. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers that compromise your privacy.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the data we collect, please contact us through our official support channels.

Your privacy is important to us.
